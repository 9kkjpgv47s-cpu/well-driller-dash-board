1. What this does (big picture)

ChatGPT on its own does not know anything about Indiana’s Department of Natural Resources (DNR) water-well records. The Driller Hub does. By “connecting” the two, you let ChatGPT ask the Driller Hub questions on your behalf — for example, “how many wells are near this address and how deep are they?” — and get real answers from the state registry instead of guesses.

The connection uses an open standard called MCP (Model Context Protocol). Think of it as a phone line between ChatGPT and the Driller Hub. You set up the line once; after that, ChatGPT can call the hub whenever your question needs well data.

2. Before you start: checklist

  • A paid ChatGPT plan: Plus or Pro. The free plan cannot add custom connectors. Business/Enterprise/Edu plans also work, but are not required — they only matter for tools that write data, and Driller Hub has none.
  • A computer with a web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). The setup must be done on the website chatgpt.com — the iPhone/Android app does not have the setup screen. Once set up, you can use it elsewhere.
  • An email address you can check — you may receive a verification code when you create your Driller Hub login.
  • Pop-ups allowed for chatgpt.com (the sign-in screen sometimes opens in a pop-up window).

Setup steps

1

Sign in to ChatGPT on the web

What to do

Open your web browser, go to chatgpt.com, and sign in with the account that has your Plus or Pro plan.

Where it is

If you’re not signed in, the Log in button is in the top-right corner. Once signed in, your name/profile picture appears in the bottom-left corner of the left sidebar.

Why

The connector is attached to your ChatGPT account, so you must be signed in to the right one.

How to confirm your plan

Click your profile picture (bottom-left) → Settings → Account. It should say Plus or Pro. If it says Free, upgrade first (bottom-left → Upgrade plan).

You’ll know it worked when…You see the normal ChatGPT chat screen with your conversation list on the left.

Start “Add custom MCP server”

What to do

Click the ＋ button (or Create). From the menu that appears, choose Add custom MCP server (wording may be “Custom connector” or “Add MCP server”).

Where it is

The ＋ is in the upper-right area of the plugins page. The menu opens right below it.

Why

5

Sign in to Driller Hub

What happens

A new window, pop-up, or tab opens with the Driller Hub sign-in page. Its web address will end in .authkit.app (currently plucky-enchantment-78-staging.authkit.app). That is expected and legitimate — it’s the secure login service the Driller Hub uses.

What to do
  1. First time? Click Sign up (usually a link under the sign-in box) and enter your email. Otherwise enter your email and click Continue.
  2. Follow the prompt — enter a password, or check your email for a verification code and type it in.
  3. If you see a screen asking to allow ChatGPT access to Driller Hub, click Allow / Approve.
  4. The window closes (or redirects) back to ChatGPT automatically.
Why

This proves to the Driller Hub who is asking. ChatGPT never sees your password — it only receives a time-limited pass (“token”) that works for the Driller Hub alone. ChatGPT renews it quietly in the background.

You’ll know it worked when…Back in ChatGPT, Driller Hub shows as Connected (or enabled), and you may see the list of its 4 tools.
Nothing opened?Your browser probably blocked the pop-up. Look for a blocked-pop-up icon at the right end of the address bar, choose “Always allow pop-ups from chatgpt.com”, and click Connect again.

Approval prompts

The first time (and sometimes every time) ChatGPT wants to use a tool, it shows a small card asking permission, e.g. “Driller Hub wants to run area_insights”. Click Allow / Confirm. Where offered, “Always allow” skips future prompts.

You’ll know it worked when…ChatGPT shows that it called Driller Hub (often a small “Used Driller Hub” label you can expand) and answers with specific numbers — well counts, depths, record IDs — for the Indiana location you asked about.

What the 4 tools do

You don’t have to call these by name — ChatGPT picks the right one based on your question. They’re listed so you know what’s possible.

ToolPlain-English meaningExample question that uses it
geocode_addressTurns an Indiana street address or place name into map coordinates so the other tools know where to look.“Where exactly is 500 W Washington St, Indianapolis?”
area_insightsSummary of all registered wells around a spot: how many, typical depths, water levels, and a confidence score showing how much data backs the numbers.“What should I expect drilling a well near Kokomo?”
wells_nearbyA list of the nearest individual registered wells with their details (distance, depth, etc.).“List the 10 closest wells to this address.”
dnr_well_recordThe full official record for one specific DNR well, looked up by its ID.“Pull up the full record for well 123456.”
Indiana onlyThe data covers Indiana DNR’s water-well registry. Questions about other states will come back empty — that’s expected, not an error.

Disconnect, reconnect, or remove

  • Disconnect / remove: profile picture (bottom-left) → Settings → Apps & Connectors → click Driller Hub → Disconnect or Delete. (Or from chatgpt.com/plugins → Driller Hub → menu “⋯”.)
  • Reconnect: same place → Connect, then sign in again (Step 5).
  • Signed out unexpectedly? If your login expires or was reset, ChatGPT will show a “Reconnect”/“Sign in” prompt the next time it tries to use Driller Hub. Click it and repeat Step 5.
  • Turn it off for one chat: just don’t mention @Driller Hub, or deselect it in the composer’s tools menu.

Troubleshooting

Click a problem to see the cause and fix.

I don’t see “Add custom MCP server”, “＋”, or “Create”.

Likely causes: (1) you’re on the Free plan, (2) you’re in the phone app, or (3) Developer mode is off.

Fix: confirm Plus/Pro in Settings → Account; use chatgpt.com in a desktop browser; follow the backup route in Step 2 to turn on Developer mode. If your ChatGPT is managed by an employer (Business/Enterprise), an admin may have disabled custom connectors — ask them.

Error mentioning “invalid_scope”, “OAuth”, “authorization failed”, or “unable to connect”.

Fix: first check that Client ID and Client Secret are blank and the URL is exact. Delete the connector and add it again. If it still fails, take a screenshot of the exact error message (including any small text) and send it to Dom — the precise wording tells us exactly which part to fix.

The sign-in window is blank, loops back to itself, or never returns to ChatGPT.

Fix: allow pop-ups for chatgpt.com; allow cookies for chatgpt.com and *.authkit.app (strict privacy extensions or Safari “Prevent cross-site tracking” can interfere); try a private/incognito window or a different browser (Chrome works well).

“No tools found”, “couldn’t reach server”, or the connector shows an error.

Fix: re-check the URL character by character: https://driller-hub.vercel.app/api/mcp. Common mistakes: missing /api/mcp, an extra space, http instead of https, or a trailing slash. Then click Connect/refresh. If it worked before and suddenly stopped, the hub may be briefly down — try again in a few minutes.

ChatGPT answers without using Driller Hub.

Fix: start your message with @Driller Hub, or say “use Driller Hub to…”. Make sure the location is in Indiana.

It asks for permission every single time.

That’s ChatGPT’s normal safety behavior for custom connectors. Choose “Always allow” if the option appears; otherwise just click Allow.

Results are empty.

Either the location is outside Indiana, the address couldn’t be matched (try a city name or nearby intersection), or there genuinely are few registered wells in that spot — try a larger radius (“within 5 miles”).

Privacy & safety

  • What Driller Hub knows about you: the email you sign in with. That’s all.
  • What ChatGPT sends the hub: only the details needed for the lookup — e.g. the address, city, radius, or well ID in your question. Not your other chats.
  • Your password is entered only on the Driller Hub sign-in page; ChatGPT never sees it.
  • The data comes from Indiana DNR’s public water-well registry.
  • Read-only: nothing can be changed or deleted through this connection.

FAQ

Does this cost me anything beyond my ChatGPT plan?

No. Your Plus/Pro subscription is the only cost.

Can several people connect?

Yes. Each person adds Driller Hub in their own ChatGPT account and signs in as themselves. Nothing is shared between users.

Can I use it on my phone?

The one-time setup must be done on chatgpt.com in a computer browser. After that, the connector is tied to your account and may be usable from the ChatGPT app as OpenAI enables connectors there.

Does it work with Claude or other AI apps?

Any app that supports MCP servers with OAuth sign-in can use the same address. For apps that need an API key instead, ask Dom for a personal key.

Do I need a WorkOS account?

No. WorkOS just runs the sign-in page behind the scenes. You only create a Driller Hub login in Step 5.

Glossary

MCP (Model Context Protocol)
An open standard that lets AI chat apps talk to outside services. The Driller Hub speaks MCP.
MCP server
The service on the other end of the line — here, the Driller Hub at https://driller-hub.vercel.app/api/mcp.
Connector / Plugin / App
ChatGPT’s names for an outside service you’ve added. They mean the same thing in this guide.
OAuth
A secure sign-in method: you log in on the service’s own page, and the app receives a limited pass instead of your password.
Token
That limited, expiring pass. ChatGPT renews it automatically.
AuthKit / WorkOS
The company and product that host the Driller Hub sign-in page (the .authkit.app address).
Tool
One specific action the hub can do, like “find wells nearby”. ChatGPT chooses which tool to use.
Developer mode
A ChatGPT setting that unlocks adding custom connectors on some accounts.
DNR
Indiana Department of Natural Resources, which maintains the state water-well registry.
